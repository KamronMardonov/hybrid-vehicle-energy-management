# Hybrid Vehicle Simulation Results

## Overview

This section presents the results of the hybrid vehicle powertrain simulation, focusing on energy use and vehicle performance under different operating conditions.

## 1. Battery State of Charge (SOC)

The SOC analysis examines changes in battery charge during vehicle operation, including partial recovery through regenerative braking.

## 2. Power Distribution

This analysis examines power distribution between the internal combustion engine and electric motor during acceleration, cruising, high-load operation, and regenerative braking.

## 3. Fuel Consumption

Fuel consumption is evaluated to investigate how powertrain operation and energy management affect fuel use under the modeled conditions.

## 4. Driving Range

Driving range is estimated using the model's energy consumption and fuel consumption results. The estimates depend on the assumptions and parameters used in the simulation.

## Future Work

Further analysis could compare alternative energy management strategies and investigate their effects on fuel consumption, battery utilization, and driving range.
