# MATLAB Simulation

## Overview

This project uses MATLAB to investigate the performance of a simplified hybrid vehicle powertrain.

Four modified configurations (M1–M4) were compared with a baseline vehicle model to study the effects of power distribution on energy management and vehicle performance.

## Simulation Conditions

The modeled operating conditions include:

- Acceleration
- Cruising
- High-load operation
- Regenerative braking

The overall vehicle mass and battery power were kept constant during the comparisons.

## Parameters Analyzed

- Power distribution between the internal combustion engine and electric motor
- Battery State of Charge (SOC)
- Fuel consumption
- Estimated driving range

## Future Development

Potential improvements include refining the vehicle model, evaluating alternative energy management strategies, and investigating powertrain optimization methods.
