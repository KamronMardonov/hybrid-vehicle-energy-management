# Hybrid Vehicle Energy and Power Transmission System Modeling

**Author:** Kamron Mardonov  
**Degree:** B.Eng. in Vehicle Engineering (Electric Vehicles)  
**University:** Tashkent State Transport University

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on modeling and analyzing the energy and power transmission systems of a hybrid vehicle using MATLAB and Autodesk Fusion 360.

The project investigates power distribution between the internal combustion engine and electric motor, battery state of charge (SOC), fuel consumption, and driving range under different operating conditions.

It combines mathematical simulation with mechanical design to support the analysis of hybrid vehicle powertrain performance and energy efficiency.

## 2. Research Objectives

- Model the energy and power transmission systems of a hybrid vehicle.
- Investigate power distribution between the internal combustion engine and electric motor.
- Analyze battery state of charge under different operating conditions.
- Examine fuel consumption and energy efficiency.
- Estimate driving range based on energy use and fuel consumption.
- Develop 3D mechanical models of selected powertrain components.

## 3. Tools and Technologies

- MATLAB — mathematical modeling and numerical simulation.
- Autodesk Fusion 360 — 3D mechanical modeling and powertrain design.

## 4. System Components

The project considers the interaction between the following hybrid powertrain components:

- Internal combustion engine
- Electric motor
- Battery system
- Transmission and planetary gear mechanism

## 5. Simulation and Analysis

The simulation investigates different operating conditions, including:

- Acceleration
- Cruising
- High-load operation
- Regenerative braking

The analysis focuses on power distribution, battery state of charge, fuel consumption, and estimated driving range.

## 6. Engineering Design

Autodesk Fusion 360 was used to develop 3D models of selected hybrid powertrain components, including the internal combustion engine and planetary gear transmission.

These models support the mechanical representation of the system and complement the mathematical simulation.

## 7. Expected Applications

Understanding energy flow and power distribution in hybrid vehicles can support the investigation of more efficient powertrain operation.

Potential future work includes comparing energy management strategies, evaluating alternative powertrain configurations, and investigating optimization methods.

## 8. Future Development

- Improve the accuracy of the hybrid vehicle simulation.
- Compare alternative energy management strategies.
- Analyze fuel consumption under different driving cycles.
- Investigate powertrain efficiency and battery utilization.
- Explore optimization methods for hybrid vehicle energy management.
